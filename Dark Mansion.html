Dark Mansion

You are alone in an old mansion. Find all the keys, then unlock the exit door (the red mark). Once you start moving you cannot stop, you can only turn. Collect coins. Glowing orbs appear for a few seconds: green ones help you, red ones hurt. Coins you collect are saved between games and can buy help during a run with the buttons at the bottom right. Turn your sound on.
